After countless requests for Luigi over the years, and because I've seen other crappy edits of my SuperMario, I've decided to release my own crappy edit of SuperMario: SuperLuigi!

He's basically the same as SuperMario, but with a few minor tweaks to make him slightly different. Also, SuperLuigi does not have any storyboards. You'll probably notice that I'm very lazy, and haven't even changed the older file names to "Luigi" instead of "Mario". Consider it an homage to said crappy edits.

INSTALLATION:

•When you download Luigi, unzip him and make sure the folder name is "SuperLuigi".
•Place "SuperLuigi" folder into the "chars" folder within the folder for Mugen.
•Go to "data" folder...the open "select" def file using NOTEPAD.
•Scroll down to where it says the following:



;Insert your characters below.

kfm, stages/kfm.def

;-----------------------



•Type in "SuperLuigi" above or below "kfm". Like so:


;Insert your characters below.

SuperLuigi
kfm, stages/kfm.def

;-----------------------


Save the altered "select" file....and SuperLuigi will then show up in the Character Selection screen next time you run Mugen.

For stages, empty the stage's contents into the "stages" folder, then in the "select" file, type in the stage's name under the section for extra stages. Like so:


stages/HauntedCastle.def



If you want Luigi's default stage to be Peach's Castle....put his name like this in the characters section of the "select" file:


;Insert your characters below.

SuperLuigi, stages/HauntedCastle.def
kfm, stages/kfm.def

This is pretty much supposed to be an improvement over ShinSamus and Neo Ankh's

of their Super Mario character, so I decided to step in and edit few things to 
make him be MORE like Luigi, than be generic Mario clone.

Changes:

- Fixed head.pos, mid.pose and height, so he's no longer same like Mario.

- Taunt changed to Luigi's main taunt from Super Smash Bros Melee and SSB64,
it does hurt the opponent, but it causes no damage.

- Special victory pose in Ghost mode (8p and 11p) is removed since... it doesn't
make sense for Luigi to get sucked by himself, especially if in intro he leaves 
his body, as well Mario with Vacuum never even exists, so... I got rid of that.

- Fixed size on Stone Drop intro, Neo intro, Tanooki intro and Soul Leaves intro, 
Luigi (and matrix effect in Neo intro) is there in his own size, instead of Mario's.

- Edited Stone Drop intro for Luigi to fall from sky landing on his stomach instead
of on both his two legs, since it fits more to Luigi .

- Position of hitsparks and guardsparks is fixed, even in fireballs.

- Luigi's EX shadow and glow in EX moves is colored lime/green, so he's not being 
so much similar to Mario.

- Special Intro's effect like glow and shadow are changed from orange to green, but

Luigi too.

- Special victory pose that Mario has when he wins the round without any damage

- Speed in rolling forwards and backwards is higered up a bit, since he's faster than
Mario

GAMEPLAY:


SuperLuigi is a six-button character, and can "chain" most attacks together.


X - Light Punch (jab)

Y - Medium Punch (strong)

Z - Hard Punch (fierce)

A - Light Kick (short)

B - Medium (forward)

C - Hard Kick (roundhouse)


He can double jump, and tapping forward twice makes him run.

Tapping back twice makes him roll backward mostly invincible.

Tapping 2 kicks simultaneously makes him roll forward invincible

Down+Z is his "launcher", knocking the opponent into the air.

Hold up directly after to "super jump" up with them to combo. Tapping down then up quickly also super jumps

Down+C is his spinning sweep kick...hits opponents in front and behind him. 

Press Start to make him taunt....which can give him more super meter.



SPECIAL MOVES:


* Fireball - (D,DF,F + any punch) (can be done in air)

* Tornado Spin - (D,DB,B + any punch) (can be done in air)

* Coin Punch - (F,D,DF + any punch)

* Air Glide - (Tap Forward twice in air)

* Head Stomp - (D+A in air)
 
* Drill Kick - (D+B in air)

* Head Crush - (D+C in air)

* Slide Kick - (run, hold forward + C)

* Spin Throw - (press punch + kick of equal strength together....ex: A+X, B+Y, or C+Z) (can be done in air)



EX SPECIALS (One level of super meter):


* EX Fireball - (D,DF,F, + 2 punches) (Can be done in air)

* EX Tornado Spin - (D,DB,B, 2 punches) (Can be done in air) (can cancel into EX Coin Punch)

* EX Coin Punch - (F,D,DF, 2 punches)

* EX Air Glide [Green Missle] - (Tap B,B,F quickly in the air) (can cancel into EX Butt Stomp)

* EX Head Crush - (Down + 2 kicks in air)

* EX Spin Throw - (D,DB,B, punch + kick of equal strength)


* EX Coin Punch Counter - (B,DB,D + Z) (Done while blocking....takes 2 levels of super meter)



LEVEL-3 Super Moves  (Uses fully charged super meter):


* Invincible Star - (D,DF,F, D,DF,F + A)  (Luigi is invincible for 7 seconds)

* Super Hammer - (D,DF,F, D,DF,F + B)

* Poltergust 3000, Elemental Medals - (D,DF,F, D,DF,F + C)

PALETTES:


* (a) - Pink color from Melee and SSB64 (Pink hat/shirt, red overalls)

* (b) - Blue color from Melee and SSB64 (Blue hat/shirt, black overalls)

* (c) - Reversed Luigi color (Dark blue hat/shirt, green overalls)

* (x) - Luigi color (Green hat/shirt, dark blue overalls)

* (y) - Waluigi color (Purple hat/shirt, black overalls)

* (z) - Fire Luigi color (White hat/shirt, green overalls)


SPECIAL PALETTES (These have special abilities....see down below):

* (Hold start, press x or a) - "Neo" Luigi

* (Hold start, press y or b) - "Ghost" Luigi

* (Hold start, press z or a) - "Stone" Luigi




STRENGTHS AND WEAKNESSES OF THE VARIOUS PALETTES:



"SuperLuigi" (Any of the normal attack buttons)

(No major strengths or weaknesses)



"NeoLuigi" (Start + X or A)
(Luigi wearing black...has green after-images trailing behind him)

Strengths:
(Hits can cancel into any other hit)
(No juggle check)
(Air Glide and Butt Stomp speed boost)
(Wall Jump....allows for up to 4 jumps)
(Infinite Guard Cancel....drains Super meter but can do it without any)
(TIME FREEZE....Down x3 when Life Bar is low....Level 3 Super)
(Careful though...this freezes your teammate as well!)

Weaknesses:
(Extremely low attack damage)
(Cannot throw at all)
(Cannot use EX Specials)
(Cannot use Level-3 Super moves)



"GhostLuigi" (Start + Y or B)
(Luigi is all white except skin, is transparent...no shadow)

Strengths:
(Almost completely invulnerable)
(More powerful throws)
(Super meter auto-fills rapidly)
(Invincible Star makes his attacks unblockable/more damage)
(Last hit of Super Hammer is unblockable)
(Gives teammate Super meter even after KO)
(TELEPORT....Down x3...brings you near opponent)

Weaknesses:
(Life Bar drains rapidly...even during Level-3 Supers)
(Gotta take them out before you "enter the light!" 



"StoneLuigi" (Start + Z or C)
(Luigi is all gray...shakes screen when landing or running)

Strengths:
(Takes less damage from attacks)
(Attacks do more damage)
(Hard Punch knocks opponent across the screen)
(STATUE BLAST....Down x3 after getting Level-1 Super meter....drains meter fast)
(Turns Luigi into an invulnerable/immobile statue)
(Any attack button Luigi bust out of statue for moderate damage to opponent)


Weaknesses:
(Super meter constantly depletes)
(Cannot use Air Glide)
(Cannot perform Level-3 Supers....unless GhostLuigi is teammate)
