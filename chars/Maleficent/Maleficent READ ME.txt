; ********** - (STORY) - ********** ;
______________________________________________________________________________

Maleficent is an evil fairy and the self-proclaimed "Mistress of All Evil".
who, after not being invited to a christening,
curses the infant Princess Aurora to "prick her finger on 
the spindle of a spinning wheel and die" before the sun 
sets on Aurora's sixteenth birthday.
