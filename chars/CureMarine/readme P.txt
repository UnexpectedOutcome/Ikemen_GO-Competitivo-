Cure Marine
From HeartCatch PreCure!

Latest version: 1.0 (09/28/2025)

Character author: Sergio P


--------- Description -------

A Knuckle Fighter X/Alpha character conversion.

As a PreCure fan and Cure Marine being one of my favorite PreCures ever, I was destined to convert her... since 2024.

Cure Marine in Knuckle Fighter has 3 versions: A regular version, a Terry Bogard version, and a Charlie Nash version. I tried to mix all 3 versions by only including worthy moves and I believe I did a pretty good job (Dodges & Rolls and Power Charge don't exist on her Knuckle Fighter counterpart, I added these just for fun lol).

Her own awesome voice is there too! Some of them are voice clips from her Knuckle Fighter counterpart and some of them are voice clips I recorded, because reasons.


--------- Move List ---------

-------------
Normals:
-------------

Light Punch - X

Heavy Punch - Y

Light Kick - A

Heavy Kick - B

Air Kick attacks have alternative versions, Hold Down + A or B

Marine Butt Punch - Y and B

Dash Punch - While Running, X or Y

Dash Kick - While Running, A or B

Dodge - Z (Air version included)

Roll - Forward or Back + Z

Throw - Hold Forward near opponent + Y


-------------
Specials:
-------------

Power Wave - Down + Forward + X or Y

Burn Knuckle - Down + Back + X or Y

Sonic Boom - Back + Forward + X or Y

Somersault Kick Shell - Forward + Down + Forward + A or B

Marine Dive - While in the air, Down + Back + A or B


-------------
Supers:
-------------

Power Geyser - Down + Back + Forward + X or Y (Requires 1 power bar)

Somersault Justice - Down + Forward + A and B (Requires 1 power bar)

Marine Shoot - Down + Forward + X and Y (Requires 1 power bar)

Blue Forte Wave - Down + Down + X and Y (Requires 2 power bars)


-------------
Mechanics:
-------------

Power Charge - Hold C

Wall Jump - When near wall and jumping, Up + Forward

Air Dash - While in the air, Double Tap Forward (Hold Forward to keep dashing for a small amount of time)


Not just that, Air Dash has it's own attacks!

Air Dash Punch - While Air Dashing, X or Y (Can follow-up for one more punch after pressing X or Y quickly, only if the first punch hits)
 
Air Dash Kick - While Air Dashing, A or B
