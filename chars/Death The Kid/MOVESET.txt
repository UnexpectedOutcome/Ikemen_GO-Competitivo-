A - Attack 1

B - Attack 2

C - Shot (consumes power)

S - Power Charge

D,D - Dash (to get behind the opponent in close range combat)


SPECIALS (Power >= 1000):

D,DF,F,a - Multiple Shots / (In "Tsumi no Kamae"): If the enemy is in the air, he shoots up

D,DF,F,b - Shooting Up the Enemy / (In "Tsumi no Kamae"): "Perfect Combo" (Moves used against Crona in episode 16)

D,DF,F,c - Rush

SPECIALS (Power >= 2000):

D,DB,B,a - Counter

D,DB,B,b - Shooting Front and Back / (In "Tsumi no Kamae"): "Shots and Skate"

D,DB,B,c - "Shishin Taijutsu 'Tsumi' no Kamae" ("Death God Taijutsu: 'Crime'/'Guilty'/'Sin' Stance") - Unlocks new Movements for some Specials during some time

SUPER (Power >= 3000):

x - "Soul Resonance": Death Cannon

Some atacks were created based on Kid's moves in the anime series, and some were invented. The dialogs also came from the anime series.
