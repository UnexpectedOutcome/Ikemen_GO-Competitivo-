
======================================================================================================================================================
System Mechanics: ====================================================================================================================================
======================================================================================================================================================
P - X		|Punch
K - A		|Kick
G - Y		|Grab/Throw
T - B		|Tactic (Taunt/Cancel)
======================================================================================================================================================

Normal Attacks:
Use the direction pad along with the P or K button to perform a different variety of useful attacks.

Taunting:
Use taunts to either boost your health or gain power meter.

Canceling:
When you make contact with your opponent and have at least one level of super meter press T (B button) to cancel the attack.
