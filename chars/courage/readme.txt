INFO:
================================================================

Courage is the series' protagonist and an overly frightened pink pet dog who lives in Nowhere, Kansas. He was abandoned as a puppy after his parents were sent
into outer space, but was adopted by Muriel Bagge. Her husband Eustace regularly mistreats him. Ironically, given his name, Courage is a genuine coward, but still
goes to great lengths to protect his owners.

REFERENCES:
=================================================================
* While Courage's opponent has been transformed by Shirley, Lilly Laney from the 1991 short film "When Lilly Laney Moved In" and the Dirty Birdy from the titular 1994 short film are the transformation bases have been caused by Courage's Shirley helper. The fact is they were both created y John R. Dilworth, who famously worked on both of these animated shorts.
* His "Iya-Iya" dance was inspired from the Cartoon Cartoons' Holiday Special, aired from 2000/or was similar to Snoopy's dance.
* While he gets into an Snopp Dog rap, the song that Snopp Dogg sang was "Who Am I (Snopp Dog)?" as a satire.
* He has done a laugh, in a similar style of Duck Hunt Dog's snicker for his victory pose.
* The 11th pallete was also based from 1996 Courage promo by StretchFilms: https://youtu.be/-D2zdmaaq0g
* The hyper background has an inspiration from O.K K.O: Let's Be Heroes' Crossover Nexus episode.
