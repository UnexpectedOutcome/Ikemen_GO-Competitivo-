For this section, use the following as a guide:

F = Forward
B = Back
U = Up
D = Down

LP = Light Slice
MP = Medium Slice
HP = Heavy Slice
LK = Turn

S = Start

I'm sure this was unneccesary, but I put it in anyway :)

THIS IS A FOUR(4)-BUTTON CHARACTER, GIGI SIMPLY DOES NOT
HAVE ENOUGH BASIC ATTACKS FOR SIX(6) BUTTONS.  YOU'LL
NOTICE THAT THE LK BUTTON ACTIVATES THE TURNING COMMAND...

GiGi has two faces.  The Blue side is the "male face" and the Red side is the "female face".
