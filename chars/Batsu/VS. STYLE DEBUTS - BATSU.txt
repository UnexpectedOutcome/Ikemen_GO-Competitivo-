================================ WHO ARE BATSU? =========================================

You watched any "Shõnen" animes? Batsu is a very example of Shõnen protagonist XD

Batsu Ichimonji, the main hero and principal protagonist of Rival Schools story, and also the mascot of the franchise alongside with Akira.

He is a transfered student[ops! Kill la Kill intensifies] from another school, who have his mother kidnapped and wants vengeance[EHH!? RYUKO MATOI GAINED A RIVAL!! xD].


An a absolutely hotheaded guy, he aren't so smart and is very dumb sometimes, and very pervert, like much of shonen heroes. But in certain moments his full of justice heart shines and he helps anyone who needs assistance. A good and sincere friend, who makes everything to protect the ones who he cares.

On Taiyo school, he meets Hinata and Kyosuke.

On start, his relationship with these two was a disastrous team up, since Batsu thinks Hinata only helps Batsu for curiosity and Kyosuke looks a "arrogant" guy. 

But during the events of RS, his relationship with the two allies turned a unbeatable friendship. Batsu now sees Hinata a valuable support and a good companion best friend. He [like Hinata] don't admit, but he has feelings for her.

And Batsu sees Kyosuke as a best friend/brother, especially after they discovered to share the same blood/lineage, the "Imawano" clan. [A nice headcanon from mine, is.. After the Project Justice events, Kyosuke now lives on Batsu house as a family member, with Shizaku[Batsu's Mother] and Raizo[The "Father of Year"]
