------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Movelist 
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

x = Light Punch
y = Strong Punch
z = -none-
a = Light Kick
b = Strong Kick
c = Powercharge
start = taunt

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Normals
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Air Jump 		= press up while in the air
Air Dash 		= F ,F or B ,B  while in the air
Hammer Fist*		= F + y 
Thrust Kick*		= F + b (gains armor during startup if used alone)
Launcher Uppercut	= y + b
Throw			= F + y when close to enemy

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Specials
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Dora Cannon**		= D, DF, F + x/y 
Dora Impact**		= D, DB, B + x/y
Dora Elbow Strike	= F, D, DF + x/y (Can be done in the air)
Dora Rush**		= D, DF, F + a/b
Dora Warp Forward	= D, D + x/y
Dora Warp Backward	= D, D + a/b
Guard Counter 		= press y+b during blockstun (costs 1000 EN)

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Supers
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Majin Cannon		= D, DF, F, D, DF, F + x/y (costs 1000 EN)
Kashinjiba		= D, DB, B, D, DB, B + x/y (costs 1000 EN)
Gadget Storm		= D, DB, B, D, DF, F + x/y (costs 1000 EN)
Shingaku Rush		= D, DB, B, D, DF, F + a/b (costs 1000 EN)
Extravagant Embrace	= x, x, F, a, y (costs 3000 EN)
FK Burst		= F, B, DB, D, DF, F + a/b (costs 3000 EN)
Dora Reload!		= Automatically occurs	when Doraemon Gets KOed with at least 3000 EN

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
*If used during a combo, a faster/weaker version will be used instead.
**Can be chained to supers, doing so will trigger the Overlimit ver. (costs 2000 EN)
