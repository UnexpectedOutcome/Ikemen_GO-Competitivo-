PAC-MAN HUNGERS FOR BATTLE!

PAC-MAN from the Pac-Man series by Namco.

PAC-MAN is a popular video game mascot in the most famous arcade game created by Namco in 1980.
His ability is to gobble up pac-dots while avoiding harmful ghosts, but he can chomp them down after he ate a power pellet.


AI Options
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Pac-Man/Pac-Man.def = Stronger AI
Pac-Man_Moderate = Normal AI
Pac-Man/Pac-Man_Easy.def = Easy AI


Abilities
-----------------------------------------------------------
Dodge Roll:                       B, F to roll right/F, B to roll left

Recovery Roll:                    Hold F/B when knocked down

Air Dash:                         F, F/B, B while in air

Super Jump:                       D, U (Great for combos)

Galaga Grab (Throw):              Hold F, and y+b while close to the opponent (You can throw left/right/upwards or down or press a or x when grabbed)


Special Moves
-----------------------------------------------------------
Pac-Dash:                    F, F, a 

Butt-Bounce:                 Hold D, a (In air) (Do this three times to create a short shock wave)

Rev Roll:                    F, F, x (The longer you hold, the greater damage)

Blinky:                      D, DF, F, a

Inky:                        D, DF, F, b

Pinky & Clyde:               D, DF, F, c

Bonus Fruit:                 D, DF, F, x/y/z  (The longer you hold, the stronger object you throw. Can be done in air)

Pac-Jump:                    D, DB, B, a (Can be done in air)

Fire Hydrant:                D, DB, B, b (Pushes opponents away, but it can kill you when hit)

Single Galaxian Posse:       D, DB, B, c (Each ship has different abilities. They can be defeated)

Pac-Dot Trail:               D, DB, B, x/y/z (Can be done in air)


Hyper Moves (Level 1)
-----------------------------------------------------------
Super Pac-Man:               D, DF, F, a+b

Galaxian Posse:              D, DF, F, x+y

Mappy:                       D, DB, B, a+b

The Legend of Valkyrie:      D, DB, B, x+y


Hyper Moves (Level 2)
-----------------------------------------------------------
Dig-Dug:                     D, DF, F, c+z

Special Flag:                D, DB, B, c+z (Based on Smash Bros for 3DS/Wii U, but it can restore your full health. Life must be less or equal to 250 to use that)


Fatal Move (Level 3) (WARNING!!!)
-----------------------------------------------------------
D, DF, F, D, DF, F, s = Death Pallet (Costs 3000 Power. Life must be less or equal to 250 to use that)
