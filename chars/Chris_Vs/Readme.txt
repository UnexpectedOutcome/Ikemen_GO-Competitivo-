Thank you for download this character. "Chris Redfield" is a character property of Capcom,
from the Resident Evil series. This character was developed based on the Versus Series System.

Movelist:

+ Command Attacks:
  - Low Shot				[F + MP]
  - Stun Rod				[F + HP]
  - Flamethrower			[B + HP]
  - Air Low Shot			Air [D + HP]

+ Special Attack:
  - Gun Fire [AIR OK]			[D, DF, F + P]
  - Grenade Toss 			[F, D, DF + P]
  - Combination Punch 			[D, DB, B + P]
    - Magnum Combo 			After Combination Punch M: [HP]

+ Hyper Attacks:
  - Sweep Combo	(LEVEL 1)		[F, D, DF + PP]
  - Satellite Laser (LEVEL 3)		[D, DB, B + PP]
