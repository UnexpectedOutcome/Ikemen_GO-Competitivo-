Borewood's MUGEN story:

Some MUGEN stuff authors quitted MUGEN then later ate their words and returned.  I am no exception, for I am not a saint.
I didn't plan to return until one day I saw a phrase "Sharing is Caring" somewhere.  I remember this phrase was used by
MUGEN Free For All.  I suddenly found out that I still care to share.

I was once a download-only member in two well-known and popular MUGEN forums (MUGEN Infantry and MUGEN Fighter Guild). 
After some long years, I decided to try to contribute something back to the MUGEN scene.  

In late 2010 I began to learn to make MUGEN stuff and to post my MUGEN release topics in those two MUGEN forums.  After about half a year people started from dislike to hate my MUGEN ideas and releases.  I thought my MUGEN ideas and releases were cool but then turned out to be uncool.  My MUGEN stuff was considered and bashed as inaccurate, half-assed, plain, and boring, while I kept defending myself against everybody.  My over-reactions resulted in forum bans.

In late 2011 I kind of rage quitted from MUGEN and destroyed all my MUGEN data.  I just failed to realize and admit my 
bad MUGEN authoring skill and MUGEN attitude.  I should have had labelled my MUGEN stuff half-assedly boring in the first 
place so that it could make people think twice before trying my MUGEN stuff and that I wouldn't have to care much about people's comments and to argue with them at all.

Now that I plan to return, I must need a new MUGEN home.  I choose MUGEN Free For All because of that "Sharing is Caring" 
phrase.  If you don't mind my bad MUGEN reputation, then let me make some new MUGEN friends here.  I will continue to make and 
release boring MUGEN stuff though.  Like it or hate it, I won't care anymore.  Nonetheless, I will try my best 
to fix my new MUGEN stuff's error reported if it produces MUGEN debug flood or makes your MUGEN crash.

About my new MUGEN stuff, it is again boring characters like before -- mains, rivals, sub-bosses, final bosses.  
I prefer to "mugenize" characters that have not yet been converted to MUGEN or that have fewer MUGEN verions, hence some people 
may describe my preference as a "me-first wanna-be" accuse.  Due to this ridiculous accuse, I say all my new MUGEN stuff is 
open-sourced from now on so if you want to start to "mugenize" a new version then you can DIY with my MUGEN stuff files and share 
with your MUGEN fellows.

Lastly, I have totally moved to MUGEN1.0 that means I won't make WinMUGEN version anymore.  

I will see how long my MUGEN return will last............

Thank you for your time!

Borewood 2013
