Runaway Girl aka Anne by GTAguy. Anne (?? An) is a minor character appearing in Stardust Crusaders. Joining the Joestar Group before departing for Singapore, she somehow follows them from there to Pakistan and appears regularly alongside the main characters.

Bio: Anne wanted to travel the world, believing that posing as a boy would make it easier. 
She believed that once she had grown enough she would need to start wearing a bra, making it difficult to pose as a guy at that point. 
She then runs away, claiming that the age she is now is the best to travel.

Bio in the mugen arc: After saying goodbye and goodluck to Jotaro to save his mother's life, Anne had a great adventure on her journey.
She fall asleep in a deep slumber and thus begins her adventure alone with her 'familar' stand in which the real world she doesn't have one.

Allies: Jotaro, Kakyoin, Polnareff, Avdol, Joseph and Iggy.

Enemies: Impostor Captain Tennille, Forever, Rubber Soul, ZZ and other minions.

Likes: Traveling, Ice Cream, Jotaro, Coconut

Dislike: Getting orders, getting touch on her chest

Alignment: Good
