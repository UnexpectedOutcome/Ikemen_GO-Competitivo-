ASHA SLASHES IN!

Asha from the Monster World IV!

Asha is a young, green-haired protagonist worn in Arabian style clothing who can hear
the pleas of the Elemental Spirits from the Monster World according to the senses to existance of spirits.
She can ultimately become a true warrior and her objective is to free them from danger.

Abilities
-----------------------------------------------------------
Super Jump:                   D,U (Great for combos)

Throw:                        Hold F, and y+z while close to the opponent


Special Moves
-----------------------------------------------------------
Airborne Assault:                 D, DF, F, a/x

Pepelogoo Assist:                 D, DF, F, b/y 

Bomb Trap:                        D, DF, F, c/z


Hyper Moves (Level 1)
-----------------------------------------------------------
Magical Hit:                      D, DF, F, a+b

Pepelogoo Puff:                   D, DF, F, x+y


AI Options
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Asha = Normal AI
Asha/Asha_Easy.def = Easy AI
Asha/Asha10.def = Stronger AI
