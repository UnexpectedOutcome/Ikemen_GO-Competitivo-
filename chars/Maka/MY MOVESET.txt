A - Attack 1

B - Attack 2

C - Attack 3

S - Power Charge

D,D - Dash (to get behind the opponent in close range combat)


SPECIALS (Power >= 1000):

D,DF,F,a - Projectile

D,DF,F,b - Quick Combo

D,DF,F,c - Rush

SPECIALS (Power >= 2000):

D,DB,B,a - Counter

D,DB,B,b - Multiple Attacks

D,DB,B,c - "Kuroi Chi" - Improve attack and defense for a while (some other specials receive new speaks).

SUPER (Power >= 3000):

z - "Soul Resonance"

Almost all atacks were invented, but the dialogs came from the anime series and the PlayStation game "Soul Eater: Battle Resonance", and also some other chars.
