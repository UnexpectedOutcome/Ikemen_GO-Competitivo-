--------------
What The Fuck?
--------------

Okay Allow me to explain.

Yes, we all know Naraku dies. Even before he actually does die, we know he's going
to die eventually, not only because almost every villain always dies, but the mere
fact that he doesn't show himself anywhere in the Present Day (according to the 
Inuyasha canon).

Irregardless, I've provided two half-assed storylines to explain this monstrousity
we call "Cyber" Naraku. Heh heh... he said Cyber.. heh heh.. huhuh.. huhuh.

-----------------------------------------------
Half Assed Storyline #1: Continuity Kept Intact
-----------------------------------------------

==============================================================================
WARNING! KEEP SCROLLING IF YOU HAVE NOT READ THE ACTUAL INUYASHA MANGA ENDING.
OTHERWISE, KEEP READING.
==============================================================================

At the end of the manga, we know Kagome fires an arrow which kills Naraku after
99.9% of his body was obliterated by Sesshomaru's poison. We know that Naraku was
transported inside the jewel after he dies, and intended to bring Kagome along with
him. We know that Magatsuhi's plan was to use Naraku and Kagome to take the places
of himself and Midoriko, allowing himself to be freed from the jewel while Naraku
and Kagome duke it out for the rest of eternity. We know Kagome saw past this and
wished for the jewel to disappear from the world forever. And it did.

According to the law of Conservation of Mass-Energy, matter is neither created nor
destroyed. When the jewel was wished away from the world, this would theorhetically 
mean that the matter composing the jewel and everything inside it would need to be
detsroyed, thus breaking the law. Instead, the jewel took the literal meaning of 
the wish, which was to disappear from "this world." It did so, but to keep the laws
of physics intact, the jewel's matter was simply transported to another dimension.
We can call this dimension "MUGEN".

Irregardless, for whatever reason, Naraku was freed from the jewel. Somehow he manages
to survive, possibly as a result of Magatsuhi no longer needing him. Stranded in this
new world mimicing the late 20th/early 21st century, with no demons left for Naraku
to absorb into his body, this would mean his eventual end. As a last ditch effort, he
absorbs the only resource available to him: machines. Thus, Cyber Naraku was born.

==============================================================================
THE SPOILERS END HERE
==============================================================================

-----------------------------------------------
Half Assed Storyline #2: Fuck Continuity
-----------------------------------------------

Cyber Naraku was originally a computer program designed for the purposes of bugtesting
a new Operating System, developed by a lone individual. However, this same program contained
a serious bug involving network access. The program would shut down any virus scan programs.
This was only brought to the developer's attention when the computer recieved a virus through
a torrent containing the third season of the Inuyasha anime. When the program became infected,
it somehow gained sentience, as the virus and the program code merged. This newly born 
program also merged the coding of the .avi files contained within the torrent, and assumed
the identity of Naraku. As a result, this new program, dubbing itself DOS-IY204.exe, emulated
Naraku's personality, and now exhibits traits common to a computer virus, but with artificial
sentience.

Moves
----------

A: Soul Body
   - Launches Holographic projections outward. These can be blocked.

B: Saimyosho Spawn
   - Summons 3 insects which will home in on the opponent. The insects can be hit and 
     killed.
   - At 50% health, the insects will fly faster.

C: Nova Strike (requires 1000 power)
   - An invincible dashing attack.
   - NOTE: The AI can use this move without the 1000 power requirement

X: Satellite Laser
   - Fill the room with 3 vertical lasers. The position is done at random. Cannot be
     blocked
   - If done at 50% health, 3 horizontal lasers will scroll downward. The first two
     can be ducked under, but the last one must be jumped over.

Y: Palm Laser 
   - Creates two beams from his hand which sweep the stage.

Z: Genocide Grid (requires 1000 power & 50% health or less)
   - Summons a grid that will follow the opponent. Cannot be blocked.
   - NOTE: The AI can use this move without the 1000 power requirement

Start: Teleport

C+Z: Energy Boost (requires 3000 power)
   - Naraku becomes invulnerable while he restores approximately one third of his lifebar.
     NOTE: The CPU can use this move without the power restriction, but will only
           use it once per round.

-----------------
2nd Form Attacks:
-----------------

Although his 2nd form is AI only, here's an overview of what he has:

* Nuclear Blitzkreig [Alpha] & [Beta]
   - Opens a match on the main body which launches ten missiles. Depending on which
     version of the move is used, the missiles behave differently.
	[Alpha]: Launches 10 missiles one after another, which fall in random locations.
        	The missiles themselves do not deal damage.
	[Beta]: Launches 10 missiles in groups of two. These missiles lock on the the
		target, creating a pincer attack. Both the missiles and the explosion
		deal damage, but the missiles can be destroyed. 

* Optical Death
   - Same as the 1st form's Palm Laser. Is able to combo into this from Genocide Laser 
     when at half health

* Genocide Laser
   - A large satellite targets the ground and aims towards the opponent, firing in 
     intervals. At half health, Naraku is able to combo this move into Optical Death.

* Gencoide Grid
   - Same as the first form's version

* Organic Drainpipe
   - Fires two crosshair targets which will aim themselves at the opponent. If caught, 
     Naraku launches an organic tentacle from the main body which will drain the
     opponent's health and add it to his own. 

* Crimson Barrier
   - Once Naraku is below half health, he will activate a red shield surrounding his
     body. Like the first form's green barrier, Naraku becomes immune to projectiles,
     but projectiles will be absorbed and heal Naraku instead of being reflected. This
     barrier also cannot be broken, but will allow non-projectile moves to still hurt
     Naraku.
