---=INTRODUCTION=------------

Princess Bonnibel Bubblegum is the benevolent ruler of the Candy Kingdom, one of the
more prominent sovereignties in the fictional post-apocalyptic Land of Ooo, in which
"Adventure Time" is set. She is the former love interest of main protagonist Finn. Despite
her kind and caring exterior, Bubblegum sometimes displays savage tendencies.

The princess throws assorted beakers as she is a woman of science. One of these explodes.
Another one brings up three Candy Zombies as they appeared in the episode "From Bad
to Worse". Another one opens a small portal to the Nightosphere, through which a tentacle 
attacks, as the place houses various abominations. The face the beaker drops is that of
lead character designer Phil Rynda. The last potion puts the princess through her monstrous
form seen in episode "Mortal Recoil". The hyper portrait for the move is the eyes of the
Lich King from the same. Bubblegum can also call Lady Rainicorn, who is seen in various
episodes, to skewer the foe. One of her intros depicts the horse from "The Eyes". She also can
summon frequent characters Marceline and Flambo, and glide using a parasol. Bubblegum
can execute Jedah's Sange Passare move from "Darkstalkers 3", so I can display compatibility
amongst other characters. Finally, for her finisher, she appears in her science suit and
wielding the interdimensional bubble-blower from "The Real You".

Bubblegum is in the fray because she is pursuing an item of hers that has been pilfered by
Fusion Morphoids. She will not name the item of value because it is embarrassing to her.
