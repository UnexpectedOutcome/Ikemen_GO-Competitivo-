=============================== Who is Kirby? ============================================================
Kirby is the heroic pink puffball from the planet Popstar - he faces off against several enemies that attempt
to invade and conquer Dreamland or in some cases, the World!  He's also made some good friends along the way,
such as Bandana Dee, Meta Knight, Dedede, and even a love interest in Ribbon.  He also has the power to fly-
in the air and eat and swallow enemies to gain their powers.

================================= Gameplay ===============================================================

Kirby is a six button character, using a/b/c for kicks and x/y/z for punches, his jumps aren't that high, but he has multiple.
His gameplay mostly contains elements from Marvel vs. Capcom, though he has some stuff from other games -
such as The King of Fighter's jump mechanics.
Kirby has very high comboability, he has unlimited juggling, meaning he can hit a falling opponent as many times as he likes,
as long as they don't hit the ground or recover.  Kirby also has 'chain comboing' meaning his moves can cancel into 
each other and create deadly combos, it works like this (Normals > Specials/Supers, Specials > Supers, Supers > None).

Other Normal Attacks:
Dive Kick - Hold down, a (Air only, Causes Kirby to 'bounce' on hit, great for starting combos)
Beam Blast - Hold down, b (Air only, b
Stone - Hold down, c (Air only, works like in Smash Bros., he has armor while in the stone formation but can still be thrown)

Specials:
Cutter - QCF, a (Can be charged, has three levels)
Wheel - QCF, b (Can be done in the air, has armor, you can change the direction you are going in) 
Bomb - QCF, c (Can be done in the air, can only have one bomb at a time)
Inhale - QCF, x (OTG Command Grab, can also swallow projectiles)
Hammer Flip - QCF, y (Grounded version wall bounces, aerial version has a 'hover' effect making it good for extendeding combos)
Final Cutter - QCF, z (Can be done in the air)

Hypers:
Flip Combo - QCFx2, a (Costs 1000 power, Kirby does a flip kick and follows up with a big punch, assuming he hits the opponent of course)
Tornado Spin - QCFx2, b (Costs 1500 power, using the Tornado abilty, Kirby spins around creating a massive tornado)
Cook - QCFx2, c (Costs 2000 power, works identically to SSBB)
Force Blast - QCFx2, x (Costs 1000 power, Kirby shoots a massive blast of energy forward)
Superspicy Curry - QCFx2, y (Costs 1500 power, Kirby eats the Curry then shoots a barrage of fireballs at the opponent.)
Dynamite - QCFx2, z (Costs 2000 power, Kirby throws Dynamite and then takes cover
Ribbon - QCB,a+b+c (Costs 3000 power, Kirby teams up with Ribbon to shoot a massive laser beam at the opponent with the Shard Gun.)
                   (Also has special win pose if they get a KO)

================================= Palette Modes ============================================================

Shadow Kirby: Kirby's mirror world dopelganger, Shadow Kirby, is also playable as a special palette mode.  
To play as him, hold down start then press either X, Y, or Z.
Shadow Kirby has different gameplay properties from his counterpart...

Pros:
- Air Dashes travel further
- Increased attack strength on the ground
- Can jump cancel grounded normals
- Can cancel aerial normals with Air Dashes
- Hypers cost half their power
- Cutter always has max charge

Cons:
- Shadow Kirby cannot block.
- He cannot dodge
- Defense does not increase when he trips
- Takes more damage from throws

Other:

- He has some new moves
 - Aerial Force Blast (QCFx2, x): Just force blast but in the air, he angles it down a bit, similar to Akuma's Air Fireballs.
 - Rising Smash: From Kirby's fighter ability in his games. Replaces Final Cutter: Deals more damage, but lacks the projectile.
 - Raging Puffball: A devastating hyper that deals massive damage!!  
                    But it can only be used when Shadow Kirby has less than 50% of his life and has full meter. 
                    (Yes, I know this is a cliche, just me have some fun okay?)
