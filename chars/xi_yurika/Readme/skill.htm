Notes
Knockbacks must be bloqued low;

Vigor Starter and Rakujitsu no Lullaby (Strong) have autoguard;

Air Bust and Miwaku no Waltz (Strong)  and Jiai no Requiem juggle opponent;

Light Shin'en no Minuet hits twice (second hit must be bloqued low) and Strong Shin'en no Minuet hits three times;

Miwaku no Waltz is invencible during jump. Light version must be bloqued low;

 Rakujitsu no Lullaby  is a good anti-air move.

Personal Notes
Yurika is my big favorite Capcom character, so I decided to convert her (in SNK Style)... Yes, I know i'm not so good drawing, but I'm learning;

The music used on Yuuga na Hito Toki hyper is "There's No Place Like Home" - Worldwide Public Domain - Arr. Albert Marlowe (SOCAN);

Besides Dreamerguy did about 50 sprites I decided to not use them. Not because they're bad or something like that. It's just because I decided that since Yurika was a kind of "my dream", she should have just sprites drawn by me;
