:Info:
:====:

Marceline Abadeer is one of the main characters from Adventure Time. She's a fun-loving, vampire queen, who despite being 
1,000-years-old, physically appears like a 18-year-old, and who unlike typical vampires, she does not drink blood to 
survive, but rather, the red color. She became a vampire queen once she defeated Vampire King, and since then is the only 
vampire queen known in Land of Ooo.

She is outgoing and likes to make jokes as demonstrated in the second episode in which Marceline appears 
(called "Henchman") where she meets Finn and Jake and plays tricks on them by making them believe that she is 
evil. Marceline, at the beginning of the series, was the typical villain but, throughout the series, she has changed.
She is actually friendlier, as has been proven later in the series.

Marceline is also an avid musician who plays an electric bass that was made from the herlom battle-axe from her family, 
by herself.

Its been revealed at the end of the series, that she's in a romantic relationship with Princess Bubblegum, during the 
episode "Come Along With Me."

=====
Throw
=====

Blood sucking bite
z
Marceline grabs her foe to bite it in the neck, sucking its blood, draining its life, to then push it down.

Aerial Suplex
z
Air only
Marceline grabs her foe to perform an aerial suplex after boosting too high to the air.

=========
Mechanics
=========

Guard Crush
If Marceline keeps blocking attacks for a time, her guard crushes, making her temporary stunned!

Parry
F
When an opponent's attack is about to make contact.
Marceline parrys a hit from an attack, pushing her opponent back.

Guard Cancel
a+b+c
While stand guarding
Uses 500 Power
Marceline floats backwards, canceling guarding while being in this state when an opponent's hit makes
contact.

Get up attack
a+b+c
When hitting ground in a knockdown
Uses 500 Power
Marceline uses a knockdown move upon hitting ground in a knockdown.

Dodging
y
Both Ground and Air
Marceline dodges the opponent's attack, becoming invulnerable.

Aerial Ground Smash
x
Air only
Marceline raises up into the air, to launch down into a ground smash, causing heavy, knocking damage.

========
Specials
========

Stereo Sonic Guitar
D, DF, F, a/b
Marceline takes out her guitar, while a stereo speaker comes out of the ground, and when she plays the guitar, 
sonic waves are shot from the speaker. The opponent is hit with the speaker when it peaks from the ground, knocking it back to
be able to be hit with the sonic waves.
X version shoots one wave, and Y version shoots two.

Arms to the Axe
F, D, DF, a/b
Marceline spins up with her arms up, taking her opponent into the air, then draws her bass axe that
she uses to chop downward, slamming her rival to the ground, giving two cuts upon landing.
X version gives two spinning hits, and Y version gives four hits, and the attack with the axe will have no difference from the
version X.

Arm Whip
D, DB, B, b/c
Marceline deforms her arm to use it as a big whip.
X version does two hits, and Y version is an anti-air that launches the opponent through the air.

===========
EX Specials
===========
*All these cost 500 of Power, and Marceline is invincible before the attack frames.

EX Stereo Sonic Guitar
D, DF, F, c
The stereo speaker fires four sonic waves to deal even more damage than its X/Y versions.

EX Arms to the Axe
F, D, DF, c
Marceline does six spinning hits, and the low blow from her bass axe has more speed that she knocks her foe down with more 
strengh, and when landing she does three hits.

EX Arm Whip
D, DB, B, c
Marceline gives four hits while doing the move twice, knocking the opponent down.

======
Hypers
======

Super Stereo Sonic Guitar
D, DF,F, D, DF,F, a+b
Uses 1000 Power
The hyper version of the Special. An even larger speaker peaks from the ground, knocks back the opponent way back, 
trapping it in a burst of six sonic waves.

I'm gonna bat you up
D, DB,B, D, DB,B,b+c
Uses 2000 Power
Marceline uses her Arm Whip move to launch her opponent upward at a distance, and summons a stampede of
bats that catch the opponent in the air giving many hits, then Marceline flies towards the opponent and catches it in
the air, and then spins up, and smashes the opponent on the ground.

Monstrous-Morph Arm
D, DB,B, D, DB,B,a+b
Uses 3000 Power
Marceline deforms her right arm in a giant monstrous arm that catches the opponent by giving many hits,
and ends up in a launching knockdown.

Bat Monster
D, DB,B, D, DF,F, a+b
Uses 3000 Power
Life must be less than or equal to 300
Marceline turns into her bat monster form, which lasts 15 seconds before turning back to normal.

=================
Bat monster moves
=================

A - Powerful Slash
Marceline does a strong scratch that knocks the opponent away.

B - Rock Smash
Marceline punches the ground to drop two rocks from above the screen.

C - Energy Ball Throwing
Marceline spits out an energy ball that  can bounce off the ground until it leaves the screen, and it knocks the opponent
down when making contact.
