      One more M.U.G.E.N release by Gudine
=================================================
                    Puyo Puyo
=================================================
                            From Puyo Puyo 2/Tsu!

Information
-----------
M.U.G.E.N version: 1.0+
Resolution: Low (320x240)

Note: You might want to amp up the helper and
explod limits on your mugen.cfg file


Description
-----------
One day, in July 2019, I thought to myself:
"I really could try to do a puzzle game in
MUGEN... but what puzzle? Tetris...? Nah!
I'll do Puyo Puyo!"

Now in 2021, I can safely say that was one of
the worst ideas I've ever had. I barely even
know Puyo Puyo! Ah well, at least I'm proud of
it.

I think I'd rather just rest until next year
now... Have fun!
