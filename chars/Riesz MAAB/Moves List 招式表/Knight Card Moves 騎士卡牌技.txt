 ● 騎士卡牌技 Knight Card Moves ●
;============================================================================================
;━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  召喚騎士需要消費騎士卡牌點數。
  Support knight needs to consume Knight Card Icon.

  跟原版百萬亞瑟王:聖靈之血比較的話……
  Compared with the original Million Arthur: Arcana Blood......

  三位騎士能力都有強化(跟原版不同)。
  The abilities of the three knights are enhanced (different from the original version).

  有元素攻擊效果(詳細內容請看必殺技檔案內的說明)。
  There is an elemental attack effect (please see the description in the Super Moves file for details).

  圖標顏色代表：
  透明 → 空。 
  水藍色 → 滿。 
  紅色 → 停止回復(偵測到：前一個招式接觸到對手，或者正在連擊中)。

  Icon Color stands for: 
  Transparent → Empty. 
  Aqua → Full. 
  Red → Stop Replying (Detected: The previous moves contacted the enemy, or is in a combo).

  騎士卡牌點數會在每一回合的一開始重置(歸零)。
  Knight Card Icon will reset (return to zero) at the beginning of each round.

○支援騎士召喚A (弱酸型艾爾)                      [D, B, y]、[(Air) D, B, y]
  Support Knight Summon A (Foible Elle)

  召喚Q版艾爾。她靠近對手後……會向對方伸手打招呼。
  Summon a Chibi Elle. 
  When she gets close to her enemy... 
  she will stretch out her hand to say hello.

  如果對方有一定的高度，小艾爾的手會碰到對方的兩腿之間。
  If the enemy has a certain height, 
  Chibi Elle's hand will touch between the enemy's legs.

  該攻擊時只能蹲下防禦(或者跳起來閃躲)。
  This attack can only be crouch guard (or jump up and dodge).

  對少部份知名的男性角色，會說出：好雞雞！
  For a few well-known male characters, I will say: Nice Chinchin!

  具有"火屬性元素"攻擊效果(持續傷害)。
  Has "Fire Attribute Element" attack effect (Damage Over Time).

  MUGEN原創追加：能量值150點。
  MUGEN Editor Add: Power 150 value.

  消費：騎士卡牌點數1點。
  Consume: Knight Card Icon 1 value.

○支援騎士召喚B (複製型斯卡哈)                    [D, B, b]、[(Air) D, B, b]
  Support Knight Summon B (Clone Scathach)

  俗稱「教官」的斯卡哈(不是Fate/Grand Order那位師匠)，
  使用「神秘的教鞭」召喚出風柱攻擊對手。
  Popular name "Instructor" Scathach (Not the Master of Fate/Grand Order), 
  uses the "Mysterious Whip" to summon a pillar of wind to attack the enemy.
  
  風柱有一定距離的前方追蹤能力(對手太遠的話無法追蹤)。
  Wind pillar has the ability to track forward at a 
  certain distance (if the enemy is too far away, it cannot be tracked).

  有可能會改變對手躺下時的朝向。
  It may change the facing when enemy lie down.

  具有"風屬性元素"回復效果(生命值增加40點)。
  With "Wind Attribute Element" recovery effect (Increased life 40 value).

  擊中對手時產生的"風元素粒子"如果回到莉絲身上，會再回復16點生命值。
  If the "Wind Element Particle" generated when hitting 
  an enemy returns to Riesz, it will increase life of 16 value again.

  本招式大約產生五到六顆粒子。
  This moves produces about five to six particles.

  MUGEN原創追加：能量值300點。
  MUGEN Editor Add: Power 300 value.

  消費：騎士卡牌點數2點。
  Consume: Knight Card Icon 2 value.

○支援騎士召喚C (複製型法莎莉雅)                  [D, B, c]、[(Air) D, B, c]
  Support Knight Summon C (Clone Pharsalia)

  超長距離雷射光線。
  Ultra long distance laser light.

  具有"冰屬性元素"硬直延長效果。
  Has "Ice Attribute Element" extended hit stun effect.

  MUGEN原創追加：能量值375點。
  MUGEN Editor Add: Power 375 value.

  消費：騎士卡牌點數3點。
  Consume: Knight Card Icon 3 value.
