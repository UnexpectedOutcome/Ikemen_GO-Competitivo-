○真空波動槍🌪                            [D, F, b+y]
  Vacuum Wave Spear🌪

  將風魔法灌注在長槍身上，並使出兩發大型波動。
  Infuse wind magic on the spear and make two large waves.

  擊中對手時產生的"風元素粒子"如果回到莉絲身上，會回復16點生命值。
  If the "Wind Element Particle" generated when hitting 
  an enemy returns to Riesz, it will increase life of 16 value.  
 
  本招式大約產生五到七顆粒子。
  This moves produces about five to seven particles.

  消費：能量值1000點。
  Consume: Power value 1000.

○流星衝                                  [D, F, b+c]
  Meteor Spear

  使用筋斗踢將對手打到半空中後，
  交叉衝刺對手(召喚一個分身)攻擊。
  最後再使用飛天槍將對手壓制在地上。
  Use Somersault Kick to hit the enemy in mid-air, 
  cross sprint the enemy (summon a clone) to attack.
  Finally, use Heaven's Spear to suppress the enemy on the ground.

  消費：能量值700+300點。
  Consume: Power value 700+300.

;━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
○百花光弾槍                              [D, B, b+y]
  Hyakka Light Bullet Spear

  先進行一個……小跳躍劈砍。
  如果這個攻擊命中對手，將會繼續使用多個普通技攻擊對手，
  然後轉變為"魔狼騎士"並使用百花槍攻擊。
  最後再以"戰爭女神"的形態投擲長槍和箭雨做結束。
  Let's do a... small jump chop first.
  If this attack hits the enemy, it will continue 
  to use multiple Normal Moves to attack the enemy, 
  then change to "Fenrir Knight" and use Hyakka Spear to attack.
  Finally, throw a spear and a rain of arrows in the form of "Vanadis" to finish.

  消費：能量值1400+600點。
  Consume: Power value 1400+600.

○氣障壁                                  [(Be Hit) a+x]、[(Air Be Hit) a+x]
  Barrier

  在挨打時(或者當前可以受身時)打飛對手(在空中時可使用)。
  When being attacked (or when it is currently recover), hit the enemy (can be used while in the air).
  
  氣障壁只要接觸到對手，就可以讓對手減少400點的能量值。
  As long as the barrier is in contact with the enemy, it can minus the enemy's power value by 400.

  十一號、十二號色表模式不能使用。
  Palette 11P、12P Mode cannot be used.
  
  消費：能量值1000點。
  Consume: Power value 1000.
