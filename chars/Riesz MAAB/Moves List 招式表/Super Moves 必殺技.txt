 ● 必殺技 Super Moves ●

🔥代表有：火屬性元素攻擊效果(持續傷害)。
  能夠在擊中對手後，給予對手微量的傷害。
  Represents: Fire Attribute Element attack effect (Damage Over Time).
  Can give Enemy a small amount of damage after Move Hit Enemy.

🌪代表有：風屬性元素攻擊效果(回復生命值)。
  擊中對手產生的"風元素粒子"可以回復自身生命值。
  粒子任何時候都會回到自己身上(如果自身死亡會消失)。
  粒子有數量上限(超過一定數量會停止產生)。
  Represents: Wind Attribute Element attack effect (Recovery Life Value).
  The "Wind Element Particles" generated by hitting the enemy can recover itself life value.
  The particle will return to itself at any time (if it dies, it will disappear).
  There is an upper limit on the number of particles (over a certain number will stop spawning).

❄代表有：冰屬性元素攻擊效果(硬直延長)。
  Represents: Ice Attribute Element attack effect (Extended Hit Stun).

  帶有元素效果的招式如果被防禦。
  防禦火花的顏色會偏紅色。
  If Moves with elemental effects are guarded.
  The color of Guard Spark will be reddish.

  Hyakka Spear

  連續快速揮動長槍突刺對手。

  Whirlwind Spear A

  將長槍舉高並轉動成旋風狀攻擊。
  Raise the spear high and turn it into a whirlwind attack.

  無"第一擊加值"效果。
  No "First Attack" effect.

  該招式無法在對手防禦時擊殺。
  This move cannot be killed while the enemy is guarding.

○旋風槍B                               [F, D, DF, b]
  Whirlwind Spear B

○向前跑 + 旋風槍B                      [D, F, b]
  Run Forward + Whirlwind Spear B

  先將長槍舉高並轉動成旋風狀，最後再向前伸出長槍突刺對手。
  First raise the spear and turn it into a whirlwind, and 
  finally stretch the spear forward to stab the enemy.

  向前跑時幾乎可以被其他招式取消。
  It can almost be cancelled by other moves during Run Forward.

  招式第一段無"第一擊加值"效果。
  First Combo none "First Attack" effect.

○旋風槍C                               [F, D, DF, c]、[D, F, c]
  Whirlwind Spear C

  先將長槍舉高並轉動成旋風狀，最後再由上向下劈砍方式打擊對手。
  First raise the spear high and turn it into a whirlwind, and 
  finally hit the enemy by slashing from top to bottom.

  第二段攻擊具有讓對手碰到地面反彈起來的效果。
  Second attack has the effect of making the enemy bounce off ground.
  
  招式第一段無"第一擊加值"效果。
  First Combo none "First Attack" effect.

  Kuzukaze Spear
  
  來自於拳皇角色八神庵的招式。

  利用槍的旋轉力，將對手推到另一邊的指令投(無傷害)。

○旋風波動槍🌪                          [D, F, x]、[D, F, z]
  Whirlwind Wave Spear🌪                [D, F, x, Hold B]、[D, F, z, Hold B]
                                        
  將風魔法灌注在長槍身上，並使出波動型飛行道具。

  本招式大約產生二到三顆粒子。
  This moves produces about two to three particles.

  冰屬性的疾風槍。

○龍牙槍🔥                               [Hold z]
  Dragon Fang Spear🔥

○龍牙槍 / 方向轉換🔥                    [(MoveContact) z]
  Dragon Fang Spear / Alter Course🔥
  
  將自身附近的火元素集中成龍形氣場，最後衝向對手攻擊。
  Concentrate the fire elements near you into a 
  dragon-shaped aura, and finally rush to the enemy to attack.

  使用時對手的飛行道具攻擊會無效。
  Enemy's projectile attacks will be invalid when used.

  攻擊時接觸到對手(此時會有短暫的攻擊暫停時間)
  按下Z鍵將可以穿越對手，並轉向對手再次使用龍牙槍(攻擊力減半)。
  When contact the enemy when 
  attacking (there will be a short hitpausetime at this time), 
  press the Z button to pass through the enemy, and turn to the enemy to use 
  Dragon Fang Spear again (attack is halved).
