①復活需要消費"瑪娜點數"1點。
  如果沒有瑪娜點數(用完)就無法復活。
  Resurrection requires the consumption of "Mana Icon" 1 value.
  If there is no Mana Icon (run out), it cannot be revived.
  
  復活能力預設為9P~12P色表才能使用。
  若要全開啟或全關閉，可以在RizMAAB-Config.txt調整。
  Rresurrection ability is preset to 9P~12P palette for use.
  If you want all on or all off, you can adjust it in RizMAAB-Config.txt.

②能量自動增加。
  會在受擊狀態(被打)時、復活開啟和戰鬥結束時暫停。
  Power auto increased. 
  It will be suspended when the hit state (being hit), 
  Resurrection start and the battle ends.

③第一擊加值1P~6P為增加1000點Power能量值(高P模式時再追加)，需要擊中對手才有效。
  First Attack 1P~6P is to increase the power 1000 value (additional in high palette mode), 
  it needs to hit the enemy to be effective.

④騎士卡牌點數恢復時間(150 → 80 ticks).
  Knight Card Icon recovery time (150 → 80 ticks).

⑤原攻擊力乘以XX倍，會和其他的攻擊力上升效果相乘。
  Original Attack multiplied by XX value, and it 
  will be multiplied with other attack power increase effects.

⑥霸體可讓一般投擲技攻擊無效。
  為了強度平衡，會設定人物無法防禦。
  Super Armor can invalidate the general throw attack.
  In order to balance the strength, the character will not be able to guard.

⑦脫離型格擋和脫離型氣障壁顯示的字體顏色跟原版不相同。
  Separation version Blocking and 
  Separation version Barrier font color is different from the original version.

  脫離型氣障壁不消費能量值(讓對手減少的能量值提昇到800)。
  Separation version Barrier does not consume 
  power value (to increase the power value of enemy minus to 800).

⑧生命值自動回復約每隔3ticks量。
  如果生命值小於生命最大值30%則改為每隔2ticks量。
  Life value auto recovery every 3 ticks.
  If the life value is less than 30% of the lifemax value, change to every 2 ticks.

%%制極界(裏改) #12P色表限定#
  無法保證對所有角色，和比1.1版本更新的MUGEN引擊上有效。
  Seikyokukai (Ura Kai)  #Palette 12 Only#
  There is no guarantee that it will work on all roles and on MUGEN engines newer than version 1.1.

  攻擊原理：
  召喚一個Helper(助手)進行ReversalDef(當身)。
  對手的Clsn1(紅色攻擊框)要接觸到我方的Clsn1(紅色攻擊框)才有效。
  Attack Principle:
  Summon a Helper for ReversalDef.
  Enemy's Clsn1 (Red Hitbox) is effective when it touches our Clsn1 (Red Hitbox).

  不會有攻擊特效(隱藏)。
  效果會在招式類型攻擊時，和招式類型受擊時發動。
  攻擊範圍跟"氣障壁"相同(人物周圍)。
  成功的話可以扣除對手"一割"的生命值和能量值。
  Will be no attack effects (Invisible).
  Effect will be activated when movetype = A and movetype = H.
  Attack range is the same as "Barrier" (Around the character).
  If you succeed, you can deduct "10%" of your enemy's life and power.

  此外，還可以消除掉對手的Helper(助手)。
  In addition, you can also eliminate the enemy's Helper.

  為Cannon Musume式的人物Fallen Balrog的超必殺技「制極界」改造版本。
  A modified version of the Hyper Moves "Seikyokukai" of Fallen Balrog, the character of Cannon Musume.
