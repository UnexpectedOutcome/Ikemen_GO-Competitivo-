 ◆ Counter Description 打康描述 ◆
;＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝＝
  只要在對手攻擊時擊中對手，就會有打康效果。
  As long as you hit the enemy when the enemy is attacking, there will be a counter effect.

  畫面邊緣會出現COUNTER字體。
  COUNTER font will appear at the edge of the screen.

;===========================================================================================;
  打康期間效果……
  Effect during COUNTER...

 *不檢測Juggle (可空中無限連擊)。
  Juggle is not detected (unlimited air combos available).

 *增加300點能量值。
  Power +300 value.

 *普通攻擊無效 (Attr = NA, NP, NT)。
  Normal Attacks Invalid (Attr = NA, NP, NT).

 *招式給予對手的打擊時間延長一點點。
  Enemy's attack hittime a little longer.

;===========================================================================================;
  部份招式不會有COUNTER效果(即使擊中對手)……
  例如：防禦取消技、投擲技、騎士卡牌技和超必殺技。
  Some moves will not have COUNTER effect (even if they hit the enemy)...
  For example:Guard Cancel Moves, Throw Moves, Knight Card Moves and Hyper Moves.
