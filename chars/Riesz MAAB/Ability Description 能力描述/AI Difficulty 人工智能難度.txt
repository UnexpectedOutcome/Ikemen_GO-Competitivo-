  Use AILevel type AI code.
  Character strength will be affected by the Difficulty value of Options in the MUGEN main program.
  Fully utilize the AI strength, adjust the Difficulty value to the highest level (8).

  In addition, AI random value setting of the character adopts 
  the tutorial article of Seravy's A.I. Guide.

  因此AI強度將會更符合MUGEN Option難度設定。
  Therefore, the AI strength will be more 
  in line with the MUGEN Option difficulty setting.
