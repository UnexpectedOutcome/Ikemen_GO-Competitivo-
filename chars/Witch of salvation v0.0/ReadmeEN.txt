Witch of salvation. Its nature is mercy.
She forcibly sucks up all the life of this star and leads it to the new heaven (barrier) that she created.
If you want to defeat this witch, there is no other way but to get rid of the misfortunes around the world.
If the sadness disappears from all over the world, the witch will think that this is heaven.

Madoka Magica Witch Picture Book Source ↓
https://www.madoka-magica.com/tv/special/dic/card27.html
;------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This character does not assume battles between CPUs,
This character is not a battle between CPUs, but is made on the premise that the player operates and defeats the JUS character. Even if you are not a JUS character, if the player's operation is good and you can avoid the witch's skill, you can defeat it with most characters.
(Unless it's a character that dies with a single blow ...)

It wasn't designed to be manipulated, but the command remains and can be manually manipulated by setting var (59) in [Statedef 191] to 1.

a --A harbinger of mercy

b --Meteorite of light

c --Convergent flash

D, DF, F, a --Guide to salvation 
(light appears in the middle of the witch and creates waves of light with a time lag.)

D, DB, B, a --Sanctions of mercy and grace 
(Attack at the center of the player with a time lag, and if you receive it, recovery will be poisonous.)

D, DF, F, b--Deep mercy 
(generates a pink light on the ground)

D, DB, B, b--Hemoptysis of mourning
  (A fast climbing tent is generated from directly under the player, the effect is the same as the technique below.)

D, DF, F, c -- Relief tent
  (Generates a white haze above the player's head and creates a black fog as soon as it touches the ground.
When an attack touches it, it causes medium damage to itself, and when it attacks with a helper, it accumulates in the parent, and when it receives damage, it causes additional damage to the parent. )

x - Dreams of a distant compatriot, distorted loneliness
  (The witch loses once and activates in a short turn during the second round. It creates a black fog on the outside of the witch, and if it touches the fog, it will be drawn in as it is and will be forcibly taken away.)

y - God's mourning that falls into despair 
(spreads light above the player's head, causing a high firepower & wide range & long-term explosion. Guarding is possible, but let's deal with it by defensive technique or leaving the place.)

;------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This character allows 1F, but if it receives ultra-high firepower (must die), it will enter the anti-purge defense and will not be able to deal damage.
It seems that there is no such instant death technique, but please be careful of high firepower and fight.

MUGEN small story

How to operate P1 in spectator mode (may not be applicable in some MUGEN)

By pressing Ctrl + 1, you will be able to operate P1 in spectator mode.
You can operate P2 instead of P1 by pressing Ctrl + 2

It does not apply to characters that are premised on AI. 
Examples Characters with AI patches, Demon's Wall, etc.
