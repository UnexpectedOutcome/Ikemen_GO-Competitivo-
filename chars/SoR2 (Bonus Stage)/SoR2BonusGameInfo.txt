============================================================
Streets of Rage 2 Bonus Game. By: K-Fox
============================================================
This project is something I cooked up after playing too much Streets of Rage 2. I also have
always wanted some sort of Beat-em up Bonus Game for MUGEN, but I never could find any good ones, so
I decided to make my own.

==========
Goal
==========
The main goal is to outlast the enemies until the vertical meter on the right side of the screen is
completely drained. As you defeat enemies, the meter drops depending on the enemy.

After defeating enough enemies, a Boss will appear. There are a total of 9 Boss Battles, 4 in the first
round, 5 in the second round. You'll fight a Boss Battle after draining 25% of the vertical meter, then
50%, 75%, and finally 100% of the meter. After winning a round, the next round will have an additional
boss, Mr.X, after defeating the 8th boss Shiva. You'll also fight some mini-bosses as well.

The secondary goal is to get a high score. You get points by dealing damage to enemies, defeating enemies, and 
picking up bonus point items.

Remember, if you want to 'save' your score, press F12 in-game after you win the second round to take a screenshot.
Just be sure the debug keys are enabled!


==============================
Gameplay / How to play
==============================
Shortly after the round starts, enemies will start to appear from the sides of the screen. Simply
attack them as you would any enemy.

At the top of the screen are lifebars based on Streets of Rage 2's life bars. Your life bar is in the upper left.
The enemy life bars are in the upper right. Up to 3 enemies can be preset on screen at a time. (Two if the Game's teammode is Single)
If you are playing in Simul mode against the enemies, your partner's life bar will be below yours.

As you deal damage and defeat enemies, your score, located above your life bar, will rise.

Below your lifebar is another bar that shows your current amount of Power (Super Energy). The number next to it shows
your current Super Level.

Each enemy that appears has its own attacks. Some only have a few while others have more.

Some enemies can grab you. If this happens, press 'Forward' or 'Back', and 'Y' before the enemy throws you to break free.
This will also stun the enemy that grabbed you.

If an enemy that grabbed you throws you through the air, you can recover by pressing 'Up' and 'A' when
you are close enough to the ground. This prevents damage.

If an enemy grabs you but starts hitting you while they are holding you, mash the buttons to escape.

If an enemy grabs you, but is holding you from behind, you can throw them forwards by first pressing 'A'. Your character will
start to shake. When the shaking slows, press 'B' to hurl the enemy forwards. The enemy you threw this way can hit other enemies
and will take damage when they hit the ground, although some bosses can land from this. If you are playing as the Axel, Blaze, 
or Roo SoR character that I made, they will instead kick forwards when you press 'A'. This kick can hit other enemies. Press 'B'
as they are about to land from this kick to throw your attacker.

One thing to note is that some characters have more than the usual amount of Life. Most Bosses here also have more Life
than your average character, as well as enemies depending on the difficulty. In this case, the Life bar
will be layered. In other words, there will be mulitple life bars on top of each other. The colors of the
life bars are:

1. Yellow
2. Green
3. Blue
4. Cyan (Light Blue)
5. White
6-10. Grey, each bar slightly darker than the last
11. Dark Grey. Only shows when a character has over 10,000 life. Shows 'excess' life remaining.

The amount of additional bars depends on the amount of life of the character/enemy/boss. One whole life bar is equal to 1000 Life.

Another thing to note is that most enemies and some characters have less than average Life. In this case, part
of that character's life bar will be filled with a grey/dark grey diagonal line pattern, which shows that
part of the life bar can't be filled.

----------
Lives
----------
To the right of your life bar you will see "x*", where '*' is a number from 0 to 9. This shows how
many lives you have.

If an enemy defeats you, but you have at least 1 life remaining, you will be "KO'd", but then
your character will jump back into the center of the stage from the sky. When this happens, all enemies
currently onscreen will be knocked down, to prevent them from attacking you as you land.

It is possible to have more than 9 lives. The lives counter will still show "9" if more than 9 lives
are stocked, but the counter won't drop until less than 9 lives are stocked.


----------
Power-Ups
----------
As you fight enemies, objects will fall into the stage. If you break these, they may drop a item.
To pick up an item, crouch (hold down) while above it. The following items can be dropped:

Sack of Cash: Gives you 1000 points.
Gold Bars: Gives you 5000 points.
Apple: Restores 1/3 of your max Life. Characters with under 1000 max life gain 334 life instead.
Chicken: Restores all of your Life.
1-Up: Grants you an extra life.

While you are picking up a power-up, you are invincible. After you stand again, you'll be invincible
for an additional second.

All 5 of these are the same power-ups you find in Streets of Rage 2.


==========
Config
==========
In the file called "SetConfig", there are some settings you can change, some of which are:

-EnemyStrength
The higher this value, the more enemies that can appear.

-Starting lives
Gives you some lives at the start.

-Lives Add
Lets you gain lives if your remaining lives is less than a certain amount after a round ends.

-Auto-Pickup
Sets whether you can pick-up items just by touching them instead of having to crouch.

-Extra AI
Increase the attack rate of all enemies by setting this value higher. Set to a negative value
to decrease the attack rate of enemies instead.

-Difficulty
Lets you make the enemies easier or harder to defeat.

If you try to open the file and get a message saying to choose a program to use to open the
file, choose WordPad.

==========
Other Info
==========
To fight three enemies at once, select "Simul" as the Bonus Game's team mode, and choose the Bonus Game twice, so
that both team members are the Bonus Game. If you do this and your team mode is also Simul, the Bosses will have
50% more Life.

If you play simul mode against the bonus game, lives are shared between you and your partner. Remember this!
You can increase the 'Life' setting in the MUGEN options to make up for this.

Bosses are invincible for a short time after they get up. If you are in simul mode, their invincble time is
slightly longer. However, when you are fighting 2 bosses at once, their invincible time is shorter.

You may notice that smaller characters can be hit by most attacks even though it looked like the attack should
have passed over their head. This is intentional. The purpose is to prevent smaller characters from being almost
unhittable and thus able to complete the bonus game with almost no difficulty.

All enemies have a 'defense booster' that kicks in when they eat a second hit in a combo. It acts like a damage
dampener, except it reduces the damage THEY take. This is because most damage dampeners in other characters won't
work when fighting the SoR2 enemies here. The more hits an enemy takes, the less damage they take from each hit.
This effect resets once they are out of a hit state. The defense booster also kicks in when an enemy is guarding.
This effect won't cause you to gain less points per hit. You can change this setting in the SetConfig file.

Notes
==========
Some enemy attacks are different than their original appearance in SoR2 & SoR3.

The Electra you see here (the lady with the whip), is actually from Streets of Rage 3. I liked that version of
her better than the Streets of Rage 2 look.

There are seven more Streets of Rage 3 enemies preset. Two are "Soozie" and "Garnet". Both have the exact same attacks,
but Garnet walks faster while Soozie has more Life. The other five are "Tiger", "Agent", "Slum", "Vice", and "Ash".

Really sorry about the somewhat huge file size. Most of it is from the music.

Some enemies are from the fangame "Streets of Rage Remake".
