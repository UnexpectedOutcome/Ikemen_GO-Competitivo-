;========== Release Notes===========

I just release this beta version for mostly testing and feedback.
There are two versions for big and chibi. valkyrie.def is the chibi and valkyrie1 is big.
Valkyrie is 75% accurate to the game including systems and attacks. Most of the systems
works.

-Guard Crush (only on herself) will update this later
- Trick step
- Charge Step
- Weapon abilities (see glance reviver for info)

Weapon abilities are passive, which means they activate when they meet certain requierements

Her Purify Weird Soul or PWS for short is not completed. She can, however, do nibelung 
valesti level 1. Her gauge system is not completed. Next release it will be.

No AI, since i want to complete her structure until i have it very solid, 
there will be no AI.

She can do her standars combo as well some air combos just use your imagination and fingers.

========= Controls===========================

light sword ---------------- x. can be used in the air
Bolt Slash  --------------- y. can be used in the air
Moment Slide  ------------------- a
Vertical Raid ----------------- b
upsword attack ---------------- c. can be used in the air
ice ray --------------- D,Df,f, x. can freeze sometimes.
Spear throw ----------- while in the air, b
trick step --------------- while facing opponent and closed to him, press directional fwd+x,y,b
charge step---------------- while attacking, hold attack button to repeat the attack wiht much more power
Counter-------------- press w, when the icon appears after sucessfully counter, press x to counterattack


Nibelung Valesti ----------------- ~D, DF, D, DF, x; must have power level 1

you can recover life through the weapon abilities but at a certain % of probabilities.
