Version 3.0 Updates (07/15/08)
------------------------------

- Future updates will now include tags for specific updates done to
     Normal Kikyo or Metal Kikyo. If the update affects both, no tag
     will be denoted.
- Kikyo's combo limit abolished. It turns out that Kikyo does have a
     theoretical infinite in the game of origin, not including damage
     dampening. 
- Kikyo's damage dampening changed to that of Capcom Fighting Jam.
     Purification is now subject to both this damage dampening as well
     as the 40% damage decrease from its standalone version.
- Kikyo's Air Throw now performs wallslam.
- Adjusted some required sprites.
- Purification's damage slightly decreased. This was a result of
     Purification now being affected by damage dampening. Alone, the move
     now deals 95 damage total (instead of 100).
- Hyper Background changed back to blue like in game of origin, but with
     a modified feel to it. The win portrait background still utilizes
     the orange background.
- [Normal] Kagome & Inuyasha Double Attack damages increased, & now subject
     to damage dampening.
- [Normal] Normal Kikyo now gets up almost immediately after hitting
     the ground when knocked down.
- [Normal] Striker Select screen completely redone. CvS groove select
     sound added plus character dialog for the selected striker for both
     english and japanese soundpacks included.
- [Normal] Double Attack mechanics changed. You can select a striker
     for the Double Attack only during the first round and will carry
     over between rounds. You can no longer change strikers between
     rounds. In addition, the Double Attack now uses its own gauge
     instead of Kikyo's main power bar. This is mostly an accuracy 
     fix, but it does make the Double Attacks easier to pull off,
     but can only be used a maximum of 3 consecutive times (As 
     opposed to 6 consecutive times in the older versions).
- [Normal] Win quotes and dialogue box for winquotes changed. The box
     now resembles that used the Megaman Zero games.
- [Normal] Double Attacks no longer use the Super Special background
     animation and sound. The screen now fades to black like in the
     original game, and the character will scream out.
- [Normal] Win portrait changed.
- [Normal] Kagome Double Attack reverted back to the way it was before
     version 2.2
- [Normal] The camera no longer follows staggering opponents (as a
     result of Kikyo's Guard Counter & Double Attacks)
- [Normal] Double Attacks now require a full Team Gauge (3 bars) to
     be comboed into. Using the move by itself only costs 1 bar.
- [Metal] Metal Kikyo no longer displays winquotes.

Attacks
---------------

a 		 Guard Counter
b 		 Strong Attack
c 		 Dash backward
x 		 Special Attacks (see below)
y 		 Weak Attack
z 		 Dash forward
Start		 Double Attacks (requires 1 bar of Team Gauge)

x 		 Purification
F + x	 	 Ascension
B + x	 	 Curse Deflector
D, D, x		 Sacred Arrow (1000 power)

Combos: 

Chain Y, B, Down+Y, and Down+B. Doing the same command twice in a row after the 
2nd hit in a chain (or if you reach 7 hits) will make Kikyo use her Purification, 
thus, ending the combo. If comboed into, Purification and Ascension will deal
60% of their normal damage.

NOTE: As of Version 2.1, Kikyo can no longer combo from Standing to Crouching
after the 2nd hit in a combo. She can still go from Crouching to Standing,
however.

NOTE: As of Version 3.0, Kikyo can now hit more than 13 hits in a combo, a limit
which was implemented due to lack knowledge of the source game's inner programming.
Can be done as follows:

Y, Y, Y, B, Y, pause, repeat OR
B, B, Y, B, Y, pause, repeat

In the game of origin, doing this process can easily net you at least a 13 hit
combo (which would equate to a 15 hit combo in MUGEN as Kikyo's Purification
has 2 extra hits)

NOTE: As of Version 4.0, Kikyo can now combo into her Specials and Sacred Arrow, as
well as use her forward dash attack as a combo starter. This strays away from the game
of origin, but it gives Kikyo the boost she needs against tougher opponents.

While Dashing:

b, c, y, or z	 Dash Attack
