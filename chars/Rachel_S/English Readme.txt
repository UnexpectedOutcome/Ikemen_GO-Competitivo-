Mode select

During the intro you can select a mode for Rachel

"Attack Up" Press up to select. Increases attack power but you take more damage when hit.

"Defense Up" Press down to select. Increases defense but decreases attack.

"Normal" Press back to select. Balances stats out.

"Life" Press Forward to select. Greatly increases attack but you automaticly die if your 
life is 300 or less.
