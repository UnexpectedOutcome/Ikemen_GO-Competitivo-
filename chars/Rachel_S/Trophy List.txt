Trophies
------------
The trophies with this character works similar to the Playstation 3 trophy system.
Doing certain conditions while playing unlocks the trophy. There are 4 trophies.
Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum. Bronze(easy), Silver(medium), Gold(hard)
Platinum(only unlocks when you earn all the others.)

------------

Platinum: Rachel_S Mastered

Get every other trophy.

--

Gold: Without Fail

K.O your opponent without missing any of your attacks.(Attacks guarded are ok)

--

Silver: Twirling toward Freedom

Hit your opponent with F, C or B, C 30 times.

--

Bronze: Charge Full

Charge your power bar from less than 1000 power to all the way to 3000 power in one power charge.

--

If for what ever reason you do not like/want this trophy system, go to the [statedef -2] area
and look for this with ctrl+F
