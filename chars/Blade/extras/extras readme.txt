'blade2.def' is a minor edit of La Miss
Fairy's 'Darminor Outside' stage, and requires
that her stage be installed before this one
can be used. The edit is named 'Darminor
Outside Alternate'.

This edit moves the fighters' starting
positions closer to their positions in Warzard
(to the right of the stage), for compatibility
with Blade's full Warzard intro.

After installing La Miss Fairy's original
version, move 'blade2.def' into your stages
directory and use this stage whenever facing
Blade with his Warzard Intro setting on.

It is also recommended you set the Trance
Background setting to 'custom' in the
config.txt if fighting Blade on either stage,
due to how strange it looks otherwise.
